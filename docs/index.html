Unchain

A lean iOS/iPadOS app for controlling smart bike trainers over the open FTMS Bluetooth standard (Fitness Machine Service) — not a vendor-proprietary protocol, so it works, in principle, with any FTMS-capable trainer (Wahoo KICKR and friends included).

Open source · in active development

Unchain isn't on the App Store yet (see the project's own App Store readiness checklist) — for now, building and running it needs Xcode (or just the command line, via the repo's own Makefile). The full, up-to-date feature list and build instructions live in the README.

What it does

  • Manual control Power, resistance, or grade-simulation targets, with automatic clamping to what your trainer actually supports.
  • Structured workouts Loads .erg/.mrc files (TrainerRoad, TrainerDay, Golden Cheetah, and others) and follows them automatically, second by second — or build one on the fly with a compact shorthand notation.
  • Real routes Loads a .gpx track and turns its elevation profile into a live grade-simulation ride.
  • Heart rate zones done right Editable, bpm-based zone boundaries, defaulting to the Heart Rate Reserve (Karvonen) method — not just a crude percentage of max heart rate.
  • Apple Health integration Saves completed workouts — including calories and heart rate — straight to the Health app. No account, no cloud sync of your own.
  • iPad-ready, two languages Runs in Split View on iPad; fully localized in English and German, with more planned.

Requirements

iPhone or iPad
iOS/iPadOS 16.0 or later
A smart trainer
Anything speaking the FTMS Bluetooth standard — most trainers made in the last several years, regardless of brand
A heart rate strap
Optional — any device speaking the standard Bluetooth Heart Rate Service
