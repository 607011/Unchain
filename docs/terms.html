Last updated: August 27, 2026

The short version: Unchain is a free, open-source hobby project, provided as-is with no warranty. It controls real exercise equipment – use common sense, and check with a doctor before starting a new training program if that's ever in doubt.

Use at your own risk

Unchain sends control commands (power, resistance, simulated grade) to your smart trainer over Bluetooth, and reads live data back from it and, optionally, a heart rate strap. You are responsible for your own safety while training: stop if something feels wrong, keep the app and your trainer's own firmware reasonably up to date, and don't rely on Unchain's live metrics (power, heart rate, heart rate zones, calorie estimates) as medical-grade or emergency-relevant information. Unchain is not a medical device and does not give medical advice. If you have a heart condition, are pregnant, are new to exercise, or are otherwise unsure, talk to a doctor before starting a new training program.

Unchain is provided "as is", without warranty of any kind, express or implied – including, without limitation, warranties of accuracy, merchantability, fitness for a particular purpose, or non-infringement. Estimates the app computes or derives (FTP-based targets, max/resting heart rate defaults, heart rate zones, calorie estimates) are just that – estimates, not guaranteed to be accurate for you personally. To the extent permitted by law, the developer isn't liable for any damage, injury, or loss arising from using, or being unable to use, the app – including damage to your trainer or other equipment from an unexpected command, a dropped Bluetooth connection, or a bug.

Not affiliated with any trainer manufacturer

Unchain talks to your hardware exclusively through the open Bluetooth SIG standards for this (FTMS – Fitness Machine Service; the Heart Rate Service) – not any vendor-proprietary protocol. It is an independent, third-party app, not affiliated with, endorsed by, or a product of Wahoo Fitness or any other trainer manufacturer.
